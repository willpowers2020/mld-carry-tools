How to Pick Your 2DP

  1. Check the source draw pattern. Was yesterday's draw Singles (ABCD)? If yes, carry rate is 68.7%.
  2. Count carried pairs. If only 1 pair carried, that's your best signal — 54.9% win rate.
  3. Check dormancy. If the carried pair was dormant 8+ days, even better — 50%+. At 15+ days: 55%+.
  4. When all three stack (Singles source + only carrier + 15d+ dormancy), you're at 57.9%.
  5. Open the 4DP Explorer for your chosen pair, filter to ≤10d cross-state recency, that's your PL.
